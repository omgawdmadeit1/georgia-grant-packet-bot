# Georgia program map (starting URLs only)

Verify every claim on the live page. This file is a map, not a substitute for a fetch.

## OneGeorgia

- Start: https://www.onegeorgia.org
- Use for rural economic development and related equity / infrastructure / business support programs administered through the OneGeorgia Authority.
- Confirm current open cycles, eligible uses, and county eligibility on the live site before drafting.

## GVRA / Georgia Vocational Rehabilitation Agency

- Start: https://gvs.georgia.gov
- Use for vocational rehabilitation and self-employment supports.
- Confirm counselor path, self-employment policy, and required business-plan artifacts on the live pages.

## Georgia SBDC

- Start: https://www.georgiasbdc.org
- Use for free or low-cost counseling and grant-packaging help, not as the funder itself.
- Confirm the nearest center and how to request a counseling appointment from the live site.

## DOBE / disability-owned business pathway

- Confirm the current certifying body and application packet from official pages at draft time. Do not treat blog posts as the application.

## SSA PASS and SSI public rules

- Start: https://www.ssa.gov
- PASS overview and publications only. This bot does not file SSA forms.
- Never store SSN or benefit amounts in the packet.
